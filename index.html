Terms & policy

Straightforward terms.
No fine-print games.

We believe trust is built on clarity. Here is how Adintecho billing, cancellation, service access, and SMS compliance work in plain language.

01 / PRE-PAID◈

Pre-Paid Service Model

Adintecho operates as a pre-paid service. Your monthly payment is collected in advance and covers the month ahead. When payment is processed, your service month activates and our team allocates infrastructure, AI systems, and support resources to your account.

02 / REFUNDS◎

No-Refund Policy

Because your service period begins when payment is made, all processed payments are final and non-refundable. This includes partial months and unused time during an active paid service period.

03 / CANCELLATION↗

How Cancellation Works

You may cancel at any time by contacting our team. Cancellation stops future billing, but it does not retroactively refund a payment already made.

EXAMPLE SCENARIO
  • Your monthly payment was made yesterday and you contact us today to cancel.
  • We process the cancellation, and no further subscription charges are made.
  • The completed payment is not refunded because it already purchased the active service month.
  • Your services remain fully active until one month from the payment date.
04 / FUTURE BILLING✓

Zero Charges After Cancellation

Once cancellation is confirmed, your payment method is removed from future subscription billing. You will not be charged again unless you choose to reactivate. Your systems remain live through the end of the current paid month and are then deactivated.

05 / SMS COMPLIANCE✦

SMS / 10DLC Compliance Fee

Plans using SMS Automation, Missed-Call Text Back, or SMS Review Requests require a one-time $70 10DLC registration fee. Registration is required by U.S. mobile carriers and The Campaign Registry. It is charged once per business, never monthly, and is a regulatory pass-through cost rather than an Adintecho service fee.

06 / SUPPORT☎

Talk To Us. We’re Human.

Although the standard policy is that processed payments are non-refundable, we care about every client. Contact our team with account, billing, or service concerns. Each situation is reviewed individually, and where we can reasonably help, we will.

07 / PLAN CHANGES⌁

Service Activation & Plan Changes

Websites, CRM systems, and AI features remain live and managed while your subscription is active. Upgrades can take effect immediately with prorated access. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Hosted systems are deactivated after the final paid service month ends.

08 / UPDATES▥

Updates To These Terms

Adintecho may update these terms to reflect service improvements or regulatory changes. Active subscribers will be notified in advance of material changes. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

What Adintecho does

What is Adintecho?

Adintecho—also known as Advanced Intelligent Technologies—is a managed AI business automation service. It builds and supports conversion-focused websites, CRM pipelines, AI receptionists, appointment booking, follow-up automation, and reporting.

How it works

How does an Adintecho automation system work?

A typical system captures an inquiry from a website, call, or form; records the contact in a CRM; qualifies the opportunity; offers an available appointment; and triggers the appropriate email or SMS follow-up. The exact workflow depends on the selected plan and business process.

Connected systems

What can Adintecho connect?

Adintecho can combine a business website, lead forms, call handling, CRM stages, calendars, email, SMS, review requests, and reporting into one managed workflow. Final integrations are confirmed during discovery because compatibility depends on the tools a business already uses.

Pricing and terms

How much does Adintecho cost?

Published plans currently start at $197 per month, with Growth Engine at $297 per month and Autopilot Suite at $397 per month. Plans are prepaid monthly. SMS features may require a one-time $70 10DLC registration fee. Review the plan details and service terms before subscribing.
